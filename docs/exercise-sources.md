# Exercise reference review — 17 October 2026

The 16 guides are general educational references, kept separate from the saved prescription and workout history. Instructions are independently written; the SVG sequences are original FORM artwork. No third-party images or videos are copied into the application. Each video opens at its publisher or official video page after an explicit click. No external media, thumbnails, tracking scripts or embedded players load automatically.

Publisher identity, named exercise/variation and actual video playback were checked in an isolated Google Chrome profile. YouTube advertisements were allowed to finish or skipped before checking demonstration playback. Video frames and the complete original-art contact sheet were visually inspected. Playback observations are retained in [video-verification.json](video-verification.json). A source review is not clinical approval, and availability can change.

## Decisions and limits

- No original screenshots were present in this checkout. Existing sets, reps, weights, rest times, routines and stored notes were preserved verbatim. New cues are not attributed to the user's physiotherapist.
- **Barbell single-leg squat:** the name is ambiguous. The linked FITASTIC video visibly demonstrates a rear-foot-elevated barbell split squat. Its matching diagram and instructions are clearly labelled as an unconfirmed reference example; they must not silently replace a free-standing single-leg squat prescription.
- **Overhead tricep extension:** two hands on one dumbbell is an explicitly labelled example because the original name does not establish equipment or arm count.
- **Side plank:** the selected Peak Physio video uses forearm support and straight legs. A search result for Physitrack's “version 2” was rejected because its opened page described a knee-supported variant instead.
- **Neutral-grip push press:** the initial Nicole Wilkins Vimeo source was rejected after playback restrictions. Sweat's public demonstration and explicit neutral-grip instructions were checked instead. No subscription or account is required to open the selected demonstration page.
- **Bent-leg sit-up:** a publisher page blocked automated access; the public Pump Club demonstration was selected instead. The written cues were also cross-checked against [Nordic Fitness Education](https://nordicfitnesseducation.net/bent-leg-sit-up/).
- **Box jump:** the reference shows a jump-down return; the guide explicitly distinguishes the step-down reset. No box height has been prescribed.
- **Burpee:** floor-to-jump footage is a labelled example; push-up and jump requirements remain dependent on the original prescription.
- All cues defer to the user's individual clinician guidance. No routine pain scores, new exercise prescriptions or automatic progressions were introduced.
- [Hevy's workout logging guide](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/track-workouts/) informed keeping prior verified performance near the set-entry controls and making exercise guides reachable without leaving the workout.

## Artwork

Each exercise has a locally generated SVG sequence with two labelled positions, appropriate equipment and a text alternative. These simplified position diagrams communicate the movement sequence; they are not anatomical models or substitutes for the demonstration. A single thumbnail is used on library cards; the full pair appears in the guide. The neutral-grip cue is explicit in text because the simplified drawing does not model detailed hand anatomy.
